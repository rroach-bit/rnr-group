About RNR Group

We don't just build companies — we build culture.

Headquartered in the Cayman Islands, RNR Group of Companies operates across physical infrastructure, digital innovation and sporting culture. Each division runs independently, but shares one DNA: island grit, tech-forward thinking, and relentless execution.

Our vision is to become the Caribbean's most influential modern group of companies — building businesses that shape sport, culture, infrastructure, media and experiences, and elevate Caribbean excellence on a global stage.
